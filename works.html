FX Position
Size Calculator

Precision risk sizing for forex traders, in one glance.

  • Turns account balance, risk percentage and stop distance into an exact lot size, so position sizing stops being mental arithmetic mid-trade.
  • Handles the pip-value maths across pairs and account currencies, including the cross-rate conversions traders usually get wrong.
