2026 · iOS & Android

Lotwise

Position sizing for forex traders: seven calculators and 87 instruments, with no connection and no account.

  • Every figure is computed in decimal, never binary floating point — no 0.79999999 lots for a broker to reject.
  • Contract size and pip step genuinely differ between brokers, so every one of them can be overridden per instrument.
  • Localised down to the number format: 2,46 stays 2,46, and Arabic and Persian read right to left throughout.
